Rules

  • No automation. No tools for comments or DMs, no scraping LinkedIn. Tristan comments and writes DMs himself.
  • No keyword comments. No "comment GUIDE" funnels, no engagement bait, no pods, no polls.
  • No link in the post body (or the first comment). The offer lives in Featured. Say "full video is in my Featured section".
  • Hook inside about 150 characters. One idea, one CTA. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
  • DM only people who replied or commented with substance. Never send the same text twice. Stop after one follow-up.
  • Pause any format that falls below the account median after four tries.
